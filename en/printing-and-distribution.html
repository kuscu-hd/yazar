Printing and Distribution

The right way to reach your readers

We plan the way from the press to the shelf for you and bring your book together with dependable solutions. With quality printing options, a wide distribution network and solutions around special events, we make your book's way to its readers easier.

One of the decisive stations of a book is its printing and its distribution. At Yazardan Direkt we plan both around what the author needs and offer different solutions, from print-on-demand to large runs. We work as carefully on timing and availability as on the quality of the print.

We do not only print but also build a strategic distribution network that brings your book to the right readers. We work with selected bookshops and give your work many-sided visibility through online platforms and special events. Orders, storage and logistics we take on for you.

Through our sales platform Her Yerde Kitap we also bring your book straight to its readers and raise how visible and reachable it is.

  • Advice on the printing process
  • Printing on demand
  • Access to the bookshop network
  • Orders followed up
  • A wide distribution network

Frequently asked

What people often ask

How many copies are printed?

That follows the need; usually a small, flexible run is chosen (print-on-demand).

How does the distribution network work?

Your book appears in online bookshops, in partner stores, on publishing platforms and on our sales site Heryerde Kitap.

May I sell my book myself?

Yes. Part of the printed books goes to you, and you are free to sell them yourself.
