E-Book Format

Flawless formatting for digital publication

When we carry your work into the e-book format, we treat the structure of the content, the placing of images and the technical requirements with care. With readable, professional files that work on every digital platform, we make your book ready to be published digitally.

In the conversion to the e-book format we treat the structure of the text, the images, the typography and the flow of the content as one whole, and set them up so that a coherent and pleasant reading comes about on any device.

Your files in the common digital formats ePub and Mobi are delivered to the standards of the platforms, with a stable page structure and balanced placing of images. Every technical detail, including the table of contents, the links and the passages between chapters, is set up with care. In text as in image, high readability and a professional appearance come first.

When the digital publication is planned, we also see to it that the files suit the distribution platforms you have in mind.

  • Image and text in accord
  • ePub and Mobi formatting
  • A table of contents that works
  • File and system checks
  • Fit for every platform

Frequently asked

What people often ask

In which format is the e-book made?

As a rule in EPUB and MOBI; PDF is offered as an alternative.

On which platforms does it appear?

The files are prepared for worldwide and local platforms such as Amazon Kindle, Google Books, Kobo and İdefix.

Is the design kept in the e-book?

E-book designs are simplified, but the original structure of the text is kept.
