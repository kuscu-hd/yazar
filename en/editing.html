Editing

Bring out the strength of your work

Every work is a journey from the one who writes to the one who reads. Editing holds a mirror to your work and enlarges its meaning. It means less changing a text than bringing its core to light. In consistency of language, in expression, structure and flow we accompany you with an attentive and sensitive eye, and strengthen your work in the coherence of its meaning.

To make the power of language visible, we look at your work not only as craft but in the depth of its telling. Our editing means reading your manuscript carefully on many levels — from the coherence of the telling to its structure, from the tone to the flow of meaning. By building a sound balance between form, content and meaning, we keep the core of your pen and strengthen its power to speak.

With this stance, which respects the voice of everyone who writes, we do not merely correct your text but help you make it clearer, stronger and more telling. For us, good editing brings the thoughts to light, not the words.

  • A feeling for the text
  • Consistency of style and language
  • Flow and structure
  • Meaning and form in accord
  • A dependable final reading

Frequently asked

What people often ask

Does editing cover only spelling and grammar?

No. Besides the grammar we look at how coherent the telling is, whether the tone holds together and how the content flows.

When is the right moment for editing?

We recommend it as soon as your manuscript is finished, before the publishing process begins.

Can I work directly with my editor?

Yes. While your manuscript is being edited you are in regular exchange throughout the process.
