Services

Every station where a book comes to life

Every book begins with an idea and is not finished with the idea. Our services are modular: you can choose a single step or walk the whole way with us.

The first step towards the book you dream of

At Yazardan Direkt we walk the way that makes the meaning of your work visible together with you. We go every stage with care and stand beside you, so that your work is given the worth it is due.

On this journey we go with you through every step that leads from the author's first inkling into the heart of the reader. From consulting to editing, from design to translation, from printing to distribution, we give your work the care it deserves at every stage.

For a book finds its true meaning only when it finds a voice of its own. That voice begins in the imagination of the one who writes, it clears under the attentive hands of the editing, and it takes shape through the aesthetic hand of the design and the right printing. Arrived at its readers, it is a finished work.

That is exactly why we go every stage with care and build a way to publication on which the language finds its meaning and the form its content.

How it goes

From the idea to the shelf, step by step

  1. Reading the manuscript

    Send us your work. After a first reading a consultant will be in touch with you — even if your work is still at the idea stage.

  2. The road map

    Readership, genre, timing and publishing model we settle together. Which steps are needed, you decide.

  3. Editing and design

    The text goes through the editing, the cover and the inside design take shape. No step moves on without your approval.

  4. Printing and Distribution

    Printing, e-book and distribution run on the same schedule. Your book finds its place in the bookshops, on online platforms and at Heryerde Kitap.

  5. Beyond the borders

    On request, translation into English and Amazon; for Turkish works, worldwide distribution through İstanbul Books.
