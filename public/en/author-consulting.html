Services

Author Consulting

On the same road as your pen

For everyone who writes, their book is a space of its own: the most tangible shape of their work, their feeling and their imagination. We do not merely watch you write, we walk with you. With a personal approach we offer strategic, dependable and constructive support on the way from an idea to a finished work.

We are at your side from the first steps of your writing. From the moment your idea takes shape to the decision to publish, we accompany your work with a strategic eye. With personal advice on readership, genre and coherence, on the choice of title, on timing and on the publishing model, we help you put your creative process on firmer ground.

Without reaching into the content of your manuscript, we look at your writing from a professional angle and accompany your progress. Because for us the voice of a book is not made between the lines alone, but in every strategic step you take while writing.

  • A plan made for you
  • Preparation before publishing
  • Guidance with a clear aim
  • Company through the publishing process
  • A road map with an end in view

Frequently asked

What people often ask

When does author consulting begin?

The moment your manuscript reaches us. After a first reading, a consultant of your own will be in touch with you.

What does the consulting cover?

We support you in every step around the shape of the content, the readership, the development of the text and the publication.

Is the consulting personal?

Yes, every author is looked after individually. Feedback comes in conversation or in writing.
