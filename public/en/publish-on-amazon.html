Frequently asked

What people often ask

Does the content of my book have to be changed for Amazon?

No. In the translation into English, though, we take care to keep the meaning and the cultural context.

Do the e-book and the printed edition of my book differ?

The technical formats differ, but the content and the visual coherence are kept. Both editions are prepared professionally.

Who looks after me during the publication?

For publications on Amazon you work with a consultant of your own. Throughout the process we are in direct exchange.

Is İstanbul Books a publisher?

No, İstanbul Books is a distribution platform. It makes the printing, storage and logistics of books easier worldwide.

To which countries can my book be sent?

So far it has been sent to more than 75 countries. The network keeps growing.

How do I offer my book through İstanbul Books?

If you apply with a Turkish book whose publishing rights are yours, it is made ready for print and released for sale.
