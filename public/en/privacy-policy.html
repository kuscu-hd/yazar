COOKIES:

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website.

No advertising is shown on this website. We do not use advertising cookies and do not include cookies from advertising partners or other third parties. The only exception are the cookies of Google Analytics 4 – and even these only if you have expressly agreed in the cookie banner (see next section). Without your consent, no analytics cookies are set.

We store your choice in the cookie banner (accept or decline), together with the date and the version of this policy, in your browser’s local storage (localStorage, key “yazardandirekt:consent”). This is necessary so that the banner does not reappear on every page view (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). No data is transmitted to us or to third parties in the process. If this policy changes in a way that affects your consent, we will ask you again.

Google Analytics 4 (consent-based)

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). The purpose is to understand how our website is used – for example which pages are viewed, how visitors find us and how long they stay – in order to improve the website and our services. For this, Google Analytics uses cookies (e.g. “_ga”, “_ga_416G4JPBKV”) and similar technologies. The measurement ID is G-416G4JPBKV.

Google Analytics 4 only processes the IP address in shortened form and does not store it. Advertising features are switched off: we do not grant consent for advertising cookies, for sending user data for advertising purposes or for personalised advertising (Google Consent Mode: ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization are set to “denied”).

A transfer of personal data to Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out. It is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and additionally on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

The analytics script is loaded only after your active consent. As long as you have not consented, no Google service is called and no analytics cookie is set. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) of the German TDDDG; for visits from Turkey, your explicit consent (açık rıza) under Art. 5(1) and Art. 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

The data collected by Google Analytics is automatically deleted after 14 months. Google Analytics cookies have a lifetime of 14 months.

You can withdraw your consent or change your decision at any time with effect for the future: Open cookie settings. You will find the same link at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw consent, the cookies set by Google Analytics are removed. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.
