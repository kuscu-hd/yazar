Translation

Keep the meaning, carry the culture

Translation carries not only words into a new language but the spirit of the telling, its cultural context and the voice of the one who wrote it. With an eye on the coherence of meaning, style and context, we bring your work carefully to readers all over the world.

To translate a text is not to change its language but to rebuild the core of the telling in another one. That is why we approach translation with cultural and emotional sensitivity. We develop a language that suits the genre, the style and the readership of the work, and carry the text into a new language without losing its original tone.

Every translation is made by translators at home in the field and is read a second time for style and coherence. Cultural context, idiomatic structure and fluent expression are the ground of good translation; so we carry every text over with care and in faith to its context.

In this process, which goes hand in hand with the editing, the translation should be not only correct but meaningful and telling.

  • Translation with subject knowledge
  • Meaning and style in accord
  • Checked in two stages
  • Cultural adaptation
  • Agreed with the editing

The process runs in agreement with you; where it is needed, direct contact is possible.
