Turkish + worldwide

İstanbul Books — Turkish works without borders

Every book written in Turkish carries a landscape of its own. We open that landscape to the world. İstanbul Books is a distribution network that brings Turkish books together with readers worldwide. Thanks to print-on-demand your book is printed exactly when it is needed — anywhere in the world.

Reaching Turkish readers outside Turkey is simple today. If you hold the foreign rights to a book that has already appeared, you can offer your Turkish work internationally through İstanbul Books.

How it works

Onto the world's shelves in three steps

  1. Translation

    Your work is carried into English by translators at home in the field and read a second time for style and coherence.

  2. Format and ISBN

    The files for Kindle and print-on-demand, the ISBN and the preparation for each platform we take on.

  3. Worldwide distribution

    Your book goes on sale on every Amazon platform worldwide; for Turkish works we build the same way through İstanbul Books.
